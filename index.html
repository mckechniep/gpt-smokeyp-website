A few practical answers

Before connecting an account.

Can anyone sign up here?

No. GPT_SmokeyP is a personal project with privately administered access. This website provides information and policies; it does not connect accounts or host the assistant.

Why does it need Gmail permissions?

Gmail access supports searching and reading messages, sending requested replies, and organizing mail. The current permission is gmail.modify. Although Google’s permission includes broader capabilities, the current assistant integration does not expose trash or permanent-delete actions.

Does the AI learn from my email?

Connected content can be supplied to the locally hosted model to generate a response. That is inference, not training: Google user data is not used to train or fine-tune general-purpose models. Content may still be retained in saved chats and private backups.

How do I disconnect or delete my data?

Remove GPT_SmokeyP’s access through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase saved conversations or backups. Contact mckechniep@gmail.com to request local data deletion.
