To help with your request

Describe the issue and identify the affected account or feature. Include an error message only after removing private information.

For deletion, use the subject “GPT_SmokeyP data deletion request” and describe the records you want removed. The privacy policy explains local copies and backups.

Keep passwords, authorization codes, and access tokens out of email. There is no need to send mailbox contents to ask for help.

Need to disconnect Google?

You can remove the app’s authorization directly in Google Account connections. For local records or backups, also send a deletion request.
